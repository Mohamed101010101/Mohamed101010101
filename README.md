## 🔬 Scientific & Computational Philosophy

> *"In the era of precision medicine, clinical decisions require synthesizing complex, multi-dimensional networks of randomized evidence. Where head-to-head trials are absent, advanced mathematical synthesis establishes coherent treatment hierarchies that inform global practice guidelines."*

I design and develop **end-to-end computational pipelines in R** for high-impact evidence synthesis, specializing in:
- **Network Meta-Analysis (NMA):** Frequentist graph-theoretical models (`netmeta`) and Bayesian hierarchical frameworks.
- **Trial Deconstruction & Component Synergy:** Uncoupling multi-agent combination therapies into additive single-agent components.
- **Decision Analytics & Clinical Utility:** Translating statistical hazard ratios into ASCO/ESMO Minimal Clinically Important Differences (MCID) and bivariate benefit-risk trade-offs.

## 🏆 Featured Landmark Pipeline

### 🩺 [`nma-nsclc-evidence-synthesis`](https://github.com/Mohamed101010101/nma-nsclc-evidence-synthesis)
> **Publication-Grade Frequentist Network Meta-Analysis Engine for Advanced Oncology**

A production-ready, fully reproducible evidence engine synthesizing first-line systemic immunotherapies and chemotherapies in advanced non-small cell lung cancer:

- 📊 **Evidence Base:** 24 landmark Phase II/III Randomized Controlled Trials encompassing **15,753 patients**.
- ⚙️ **Analytical Architecture:** 12 modular execution engines (`01_fit_nma_model.R` through `12_mcid_analysis.R`).
- 🔍 **Methodological Rigor:** Design-by-treatment interaction decomposition, node-splitting local consistency audits, and 10,000-scenario Monte Carlo rank-heat simulations.
- 📈 **Publication Visuals:** 14 high-resolution, 300-DPI vector-grade exhibits compliant with *Lancet*, *NEJM*, *JAMA Oncology*, and *BMJ* visual standards.
- 📋 **Standards:** Full compliance with PRISMA-NMA, Cochrane Handbook v6, and GRADE working group guidelines.
